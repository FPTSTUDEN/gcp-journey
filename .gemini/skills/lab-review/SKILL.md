# Review a lab record

1. Read `TAXONOMY.md`, `GEMINI.md`, and the target lab record.
2. Proactively inspect the target record and nearby relevant artifacts before asking the user to recount their work. Use read-only access; do not search broadly, change cloud resources, or inspect credentials or unrelated data.
3. Report sources checked, what they establish, and important unavailable evidence. Distinguish observed, reported, inferred, and unverified information; an attempt is not proof of success.
4. Ask one focused question only when evidence leaves a meaningful uncertainty about progress or ranking. Do not ask the learner to classify tags manually.
5. Conclude progress explicitly as not-started, in-progress, partial, completed, or not-verified. Completion requires supporting evidence; a lab description or course indicator alone is insufficient.
6. Recommend useful tags and per-tag ranks from 1–3 based on observed or reported work, not instructions alone.
7. Make assumptions, contradictions, and remaining gaps visible; do not infer personal completion from the lab description.
